Agency Artifact

Privacy

This page explains, in plain words, what the Agency Artifact app keeps about you and who can see it.

What the app stores

What you enter yourself: your own hours, your tasks and any unplanned work you log.

Where it is stored

In the firm's own Microsoft 365, the same place as our other work files. The app is only a set of pages: it reads and saves through your own Microsoft sign-in, and keeps nothing on a server of its own.

Who sees what

  • Principals see everything, including financial information.
  • Coordinators see the day-to-day status of all projects. They do not see financial information.
  • Team members see the tasks, deliverables and deadlines of their own projects.

Financial information is kept in a separate area of Microsoft 365 that only the principals can open. That is enforced by Microsoft 365 permissions, not just by hiding screens.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read your email, calendar or file activity, and does not guess your hours or tasks from them.
  • It does not send anything outside the office automatically.

Questions

Ask either principal.
